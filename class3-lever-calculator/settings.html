✓ Unit Conversion Self-Test

Every composite unit (lbf, slug, lbf/in, in·lbf, lbm·in², ...) is derived at runtime from three internationally-defined exact constants — inch, pound-mass, and standard gravity — never a separately hand-typed decimal. This panel round-trips every supported unit and independently cross-checks the physics (F=ma, the definition of a slug) so the conversion table's correctness isn't just asserted, it's shown.
